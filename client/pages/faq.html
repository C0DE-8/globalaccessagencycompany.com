Clear answers.

If you need help with a specific shipment, our team can talk through the details with you.

What services does Global Access Agency Company provide?

We coordinate air, ocean, road and rail freight, warehousing and multimodal shipping. The right fit depends on your cargo, timing and destination.

How can I track a shipment?

Use the shipment tracking number shared by our team on the Track page. You’ll see the latest status and shipment details available in our system.

What information should I share for a shipping inquiry?

Origin, destination, shipment size or type, desired timing and any handling needs are useful starting points. If you don’t have every detail yet, send what you know.

How do I request a shipping quote?

Contact our team with the shipment details you have so far. We’ll follow up to understand your needs and discuss possible service options.

Can you coordinate more than one shipping mode?

Yes. Multimodal coordination brings services such as ocean, rail and road together around one shipment plan.
